## Hook

A year ago, AI was a feature every SaaS had to ship. Now agents don't plug into SaaS. They rebuild it.

## Who I Am

I'm Dylan, a self-taught high school dropout. I've spent my career on platforms that handle the hard parts so people can focus on the product, from WordPress themes to co-founding ClickFunnels.

## What Changed

Every SaaS is four things: code, infrastructure, support, and the founder's vision. Coding agents made the first three nearly free. The vision is the one part they didn't replace.

## Buy vs. Clone

So now even a single creator asks buy or build, and more and more, build means clone. Hand an agent the login and copy the whole product.

But a clone only copies the tip of the iceberg. The vision underneath gets lost, and the builder and the user both lose.

## What People Want

I spent eight months fighting this, then decided to build for it. People want to own their software, use the AI subscription they already pay for, and still get quality.

## SuperGrid

This is SuperGrid, the IDE for creators and builders.

You connect Muse over ACP, so it runs on your own subscription, and your Cloudflare account, so you own the hosting.

Every workspace is its own repo with Core built in: database, events, workflows, analytics, and a library, all running on your own Cloudflare.

Apps are where builders come in. Build your own or install one from the App Store, and it goes straight into your repo. You get the builder's whole solution, not a clone of the part you could see.

Admin apps help you run your business, like an Ad Designer built on Muse Image. Other apps deploy with your site, like a Blog, Forms, or Landing Pages. They share code, so a form can drop into any blog post.

Every app exposes MCP tools, so Muse can run it. And in Dev mode, Muse builds and extends apps right in your repo.

## Updates

SaaS ships a fix once and everyone gets it. Owned code breaks that. Until now you could get updates or edit the code, not both.

Muse Code knows what changed and why, because it made the changes. So it merges every update seamlessly, checks it for security issues, and fixes anything that breaks. You get every update and keep everything you made your own.

## Pushing the Frontier

Muse Code is the CTO for every creator. I believe every industry will get IDEs like this, where expert-built apps get installed instead of cloned, and their builders get paid.

SaaS isn't dead, but it is changing. Software will be owned by the people who use it, and the value stays with the experts who obsess over the problem.
